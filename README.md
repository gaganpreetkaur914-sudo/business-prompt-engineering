# Business Prompt Engineering

A practical collection of **25 business-focused AI prompts** designed to demonstrate how prompt engineering can improve the relevance, structure, and usefulness of AI-generated outputs.

## 📌 About the Project

This repository demonstrates the progression of business prompts from **Basic → Refined → Most Refined**.

The prompts cover different business functions and scenarios, including:

* 📣 Marketing & customer retention
* 👩‍💼 Human Resources & recruitment
* 💰 Financial analysis
* 📦 Operations & delivery management
* 🤖 AI-assisted decision-making
* 📊 Business problem analysis

The project shows how adding **context, constraints, specific requirements, output formats, and verification instructions** can make AI responses more useful and easier to evaluate.

## 📂 Project Structure

### Version 1 — Basic

Contains simple prompts with minimal context or constraints.

**Prompts 1–8**

Examples include:

* Creating a café marketing campaign
* Generating promotional ideas
* Creating internship interview questions
* Customer retention
* Employee training
* Business expense analysis
* Delivery delay investigation
* Employee performance decisions

### Version 2 — Refined

Introduces additional context, roles, tasks, and structured requirements.

**Prompts 9–16**

Examples include:

* Marketing manager for a university café
* Targeting college students
* 7-day promotional campaigns
* Interview-question bias review
* Financial analysis
* Profit and profit-margin calculations
* Operational problem analysis

### Version 3 — Most Refined

Uses detailed context, multiple constraints, specific information, output formats, and verification requirements.

**Prompts 17–25**

Examples include:

* A ₹20,000 café marketing campaign
* Structured internship interview questions
* E-commerce recruitment
* Café financial analysis
* Online retail operational issues
* Managerial decision investigation
* AI-assisted hiring review
* Discount analysis
* Evaluation of prompt improvement

## 🎯 Key Learning

The project demonstrates that **better prompts generally provide AI with clearer instructions and business context**.

As prompts become more refined, they can include:

1. **Role** — Who should the AI act as?
2. **Context** — What business situation is being addressed?
3. **Task** — What exactly should the AI do?
4. **Constraints** — What limitations should it follow?
5. **Output Format** — How should the response be presented?
6. **Verification** — What assumptions or missing information should be identified?

## 💡 Example

### Basic Prompt

### Most Refined Prompt

This makes the AI output more structured, relevant, and easier to evaluate.
